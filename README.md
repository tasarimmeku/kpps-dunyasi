1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how KPSS Dünyası (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

KPSS Dünyası is an offline educational app for KPSS exam preparation. It includes a question bank and map-based learning activities in Geography, History, and Citizenship.

We designed the App to work without user accounts and without sending your personal data to our servers.

2. Information We Collect
2.1 Information we do NOT collect
We do not collect, store, or transmit:

Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
Login credentials (the App does not require registration or sign-in)
Precise location data
Photos, contacts, or other sensitive device data
Payment information (the App is free and contains no in-app purchases in this version)
We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.

2.2 Information stored locally on your device
The App saves certain data only on your device to provide core functionality, including:

Quiz and map activity progress
Scores and session statistics shown on the Profile screen
App preferences needed for normal operation
This data is stored in the App’s local storage on your phone or tablet. It is not uploaded to our servers.

If you uninstall the App, this locally stored data is generally removed from your device. If you clear app data in Android settings, your progress may also be deleted.

3. How We Use Information
Locally stored data is used only to:

Let you continue your study progress
Display your statistics in the App
Operate quiz and map features offline
We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Internet Access
The App is designed to work offline. Educational content (questions and maps) is included in the App.

The App may declare the INTERNET permission as part of the underlying mobile framework. We do not use this permission to collect personal data. The App does not require an internet connection for normal use.

5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention
Data remains on your device until you delete it (by clearing app data) or uninstall the App. We do not retain copies on our servers because we do not receive this data.

7. Children’s Privacy
The App is intended for general exam preparation and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information stored on your device through standard Android app practices. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Because data stays on your device, you should also protect your device with a screen lock and keep your operating system updated.

9. Your Choices and Rights
Because the App does not create user accounts and does not collect personal data on our servers:

You can delete all App-related data by clearing storage or uninstalling the App
You do not need to request account deletion from us
Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable laws. To exercise them, contact us using the email above.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
