Privacy Policy — Quiz & Harita Dünyası

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · App package: com.meku.kpssdunyasi

Independent educational app. Quiz & Harita Dünyası is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to ÖSYM, KPSS, or any official examination authority. It is an unofficial study tool for personal learning.

Summary

Quiz & Harita Dünyası is an offline education app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with our servers — because we do not operate user accounts or backend services for this app.

Data stored on your device

The app may store the following locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Quiz and map game scores and session history
  • In-progress game state (if you leave a session unfinished)
  • A default display name (“Oyuncu”) — not linked to any online identity

This information stays on your device in app storage (localStorage / app data). It is used only to show your profile and progress inside the app.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No email, phone number, or government ID
  • No precise location data
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs
  • No data sent to our servers (the app works fully offline after install)

Internet permission

The Android app declares the INTERNET permission as part of the standard Capacitor WebView framework. The app does not require an active network connection to use its core features; educational content is bundled inside the app.

Device backup (Android)

On Android, app data may be included in your device’s Google backup if you have backup enabled for this app. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall the app — removes all locally stored progress.
  • Clear app data — Android: Settings → Apps → Quiz & Harita Dünyası → Storage → Clear data.

Children

The app is intended for general exam preparation audiences (typically adults). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
